## Known gaps

- **Recent months are provisional.** Awards and contracts keep being published for months after a process starts, so the latest months look smaller than they will be.
- **Single months are not representative.** January has far fewer processes than other months (start of the budget year); compare full years.
- **Ínfima cuantía** purchases are not published in this source.
- **Emergency procedures** cannot be identified as a procedure type.
- **Processes changed after the rolling window** (e.g. contract amendments) are only captured when history is reloaded (see ADR-0001).
